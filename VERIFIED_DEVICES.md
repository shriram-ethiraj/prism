## Test another monitor

Follow [setup and adaptation](docs/setup.md). Physical Home from streaming apps and native input, standby resume, cold boot, fullscreen fit, and restoration are independent checks. Successful installation does not imply the others passed. Firmware changes require a new configuration and revalidation.

[Submit a device report](https://github.com/shriram-ethiraj/prism/issues/new?template=device-report.yml), including partial success and failures. Maintainers review reports before adding verified results. Remove private information from screenshots and never attach credentials, raw device logs, addresses, or recovery records.
